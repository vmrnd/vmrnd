02 / Music education

Music Workshop for Students


We design music curriculums for organizations such as the Youth Global Network in Hong Kong, developed an interactive album art wall for ARTBAT in Hong Kong, and led a technology-driven music workshop for students with special needs in the Philippines (funded by the Gallant Ho Experiential Learning Fund, HKU).
